User Manual & App Guide

1. Start Your Journey (Register)

First, click the **Register** button to create your secure profile. Registration is necessary to save your data and enable collaboration features.

2. Setup Your Inventory

Add ingredients to your 'Meal Inventory'. Add prices and serving sizes—our algorithm uses this to keep you on budget!

3. Build Combinations

Use the 'Combo Builder' to combine inventory items into actual meals (e.g., Rice + Beans). Assign them a category: Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner.

4. Collaborate with Comrades

Once registered, use the 'Group Management' tab to **Create a Group**. Share the 6-digit code with your roommates so everyone can see the shared inventory and plan.
